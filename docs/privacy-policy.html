Privacy Policy for Caffeine

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Caffeine (“the app”) is a utility that keeps your device’s screen awake. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it is handled. It applies to the version of Caffeine published on Google Play.

Summary

Caffeine has no accounts and shows no ads. It does not ask you for any personal information, and it does not sell or share your data with anyone for advertising. To keep the app stable and understand how it is used, the published app includes Google’s Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, which collect limited usage and diagnostic information as described below.

Data the app collects

Through Google Firebase, the published app automatically collects:

  • Usage and interaction data — anonymous, aggregated events such as app opens, sessions, and in-app actions, used to understand how the app is used.
  • Crash and diagnostic data — crash reports, stack traces, and the device state at the time of a crash (for example available memory), used to find and fix bugs.
  • Device and app information — such as device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and coarse (country-level) region derived from your IP address.
  • Identifiers — pseudonymous identifiers generated by Firebase (an app-instance ID and a crash-reporting installation ID), and the device Advertising ID, which Firebase Analytics uses to group usage data. These are not linked to your name or account, because the app has no accounts.

Caffeine does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, files, or the contents of your screen, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Third-party services

The app uses the following Google services, which process the data above on our behalf:

  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics — see Google’s Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

Data is transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and is retained in accordance with Google’s data-retention policies.

Data stored on your device

  • Settings. Your preferences (Persistent, Restart on reboot, Automatic turn off, etc.) are stored locally on your device only. They never leave the device and are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Diagnostic logs (development builds only). Non-public development builds may write local diagnostic logs to the app’s private storage to help with debugging. The app published on Google Play does not write these logs, and they are never transmitted anywhere.

Permissions

Caffeine requests the following permissions to provide its functionality:

  • Display over other apps — used to place an invisible view that keeps the screen on. Required for the core feature.
  • Notifications — used to show the ongoing notification while the keep-awake service is running.
  • Foreground service — used to keep the screen-awake service running reliably while active.
  • Run at startup — used only if you enable “Restart on reboot”, to turn Caffeine back on after your device restarts.
  • Wake lock — declared for keep-awake functionality.
  • Internet and network state — used to send the analytics and crash diagnostics described above.
  • Advertising ID — used by Firebase Analytics to group usage data, as described above.

You may also optionally allow background activity / exclude the app from battery optimization via your system settings; this is entirely user-initiated and involves no data.

Your choices

  • You can reset or delete your device Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android system settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Uninstalling the app stops all further data collection.

Children

Caffeine is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same location with a new “Last updated” date.
